# Durable jobs contract

PHPThis accepts the optional [backend-neutral contract](../docs/jobs.md) and [verification structure](../docs/jobs/verification.md) under ADR 052. Its first and only checked backend-specific profile remains [application-owned SQLite durable jobs](../docs/jobs/sqlite.md) under [ADR 024](../docs/decisions/024-application-owned-sqlite-durable-jobs.md). Do not generalize the SQLite profile.

For an ADR 024 task, start with the checked SQLite profile and use [externally supervised one-shot durable jobs](../docs/jobs/operations.md) when continual consumption or worker deployment is involved. Preserve its same-connection SQLite transaction, bounded JSON, finite dispatch, one-row `UPDATE ... RETURNING` claim, fresh opaque lease-token fencing, one-shot worker, finite retry and dead-letter policy, idempotent database effect, redaction, statement bounds, subprocess recovery, external supervision, and exact tested outcomes. These are profile-specific mechanisms, not defaults for another backend.

When adopting or reviewing ADR 052 guidance, require one application-owned record naming:

- exact backend and client versions, update owner, topology and durability settings;
- the positive durable-publication floor: after reported success, the intent/message survives producer/request termination and remains recoverable or deliverable until acknowledgement, terminal outcome, or explicit finite cancellation/expiry under a named proved fault envelope; restart/failover only as exercised, with ambiguous/failed publication and out-of-envelope loss separate; Redis Pub/Sub, process memory and fire-and-forget work do not qualify;
- least-privilege producer, consumer, relay, inspector and administrator identities and capabilities; credentials, TLS and network boundaries; tenant and environment isolation; rotation, revocation, authorization and audit;
- publication and recovery, with a transactional outbox or another proved mechanism when an independently meaningful database write precedes broker publication; after-commit and publisher-confirmation alone are not atomic with that database transaction;
- bounded versioned JSON, application-generated idempotency identity, operation-specific payload bounds, strict stored-input parsing, finite code-owned dispatch and stored-version compatibility;
- payload classification/minimization, encryption-at-rest and key ownership where applicable, region/residency, and exact full-envelope retention/access/deletion across any selected terminal or dead-letter/DLQ store, backups, replicas and snapshots, separately from diagnostic redaction;
- exact per-type publication authority and durable non-secret tenant/owner/principal/action binding or explicit tenantless system-owned non-applicability; broker credentials are not domain authorization; before the effect the handler rechecks current authorization, revocation and relevant state or executes one explicitly committed system-owned obligation;
- exact backend claim, acknowledgement, receipt, visibility, lease, reclaim, group, partition, offset, ordering and redelivery semantics; required ordering/partition/sequence key and concurrency scope plus service enforcement or monotonic/version guard, rejection or reconciliation for stale/out-of-order/replayed work, or explicit order-independent non-applicability; where an ownership window applies, maximum handler duration relative to it, exact extension, renewal, heartbeat or session-liveness owner/cadence/bounds, renewal failure/expiry and actual stale-owner behavior; rejection or fencing only where supported, otherwise bounded overlapping stale work and duplicate-safe recovery; explicit non-applicability only when no ownership or session-liveness mechanism applies;
- the assumption that delivery may occur more than once and duplicate-safe semantic effects; zero handler executions only after an explicit recorded terminal rejection/exhaustion, finite cancellation/expiry, or bounded catastrophic loss outside the successful-publication envelope; exact effect/idempotency uniqueness scope, durable retention/deletion owner and protection horizon beyond retry, redrive, selected terminal retention including any adopted dead-letter/DLQ, replay, and provider-deduplication expiry; explicit post-horizon behavior; backup/restore preservation of corresponding queued-message and effect/idempotency state or fail-closed reconciliation before work resumes; acknowledgement after the effect or in the same exactly proved storage boundary; and no cross-system atomicity or exactly-once implication;
- finite version-controlled attempt, retry, redrive, poison and terminal policy with its application-code, broker-configuration or infrastructure-as-code owner and exact counter precedence;
- privileged inspection, replay, cancellation and deletion authorization, audit, redaction and ownership, including idempotency handling and exact in-flight limits;
- worker process shape, concurrency, prefetch or backpressure, maximum handler duration and applicable ownership extension/renewal/heartbeat/session-liveness behavior, timeouts, reconnect, shutdown, deployment replacement, supervision, capacity, lag, retention, outage, backup and recovery;
- selected service/application time ownership, representation, units, rounding, precision and skew for visibility, lease, TTL, delay, retry and retention; plus unique-run real-service cleanup, finite abandoned-run lifetime and exact run-ID-scoped stale-run reconciliation without broad deletion;
- bounded redacted diagnostics, signals, thresholds, runbooks and deployment evidence; and
- the canonical application-owned Composer evidence script `jobs:verify`, its single literal application-owned Composer-autoload/bootstrap path, and invocation by the ordinary `test` script and complete `check` gate. It is a verification script name, not a runtime worker command or PHPThis checker API.

The accepted optional `jobs:verify` gate uses one literal ordered application PHP entrypoint over publication, delivery and operations modules. It fails closed when required real-service configuration or evidence is absent, has no release skip or mock fallback, owns isolated non-production resources and bounded cleanup, and emits fixed redacted pass/fail bytes. An optional application-owned backend-specific static checker may join that gate only for mechanically decidable local invariants with one owner, pinned versions, finite diagnostics, positive and negative fixtures, mutation controls and explicit limitations; PHPThis neither hosts nor discovers it, and it proves no service semantics.

PHPThis provides no core queue, worker, job, dispatcher, scheduler, command map, supervisor, event bus, transport adapter, generic backend validator or backend-specific checker. Keep `.ai/jobs.md` in the current skeleton and template with exactly `NOT_APPLICABLE(JOBS)` or one complete adopted backend policy. Consumer Contract version 15 carries version 14 forward; Strict Profile version 4 and `PHT001` through `PHT008` apply. Preserve the starter's non-adoption; a non-adopter adds no speculative mechanism.
